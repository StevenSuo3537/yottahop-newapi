How we work

The right commercial and deployment path for each use case.

International models

We work with Australian cloud partners to offer competitive access to overseas model families, alongside responsive local technical service. The model provider and processing location depend on the option selected.

Chinese models

Inference for the Chinese models in our standard service currently takes place in Singapore. Requests sent to these models are transmitted there for processing.

Dedicated deployments

If you need an open-source model deployed in Australia, or a private deployment within your enterprise environment, we can help scope and implement an arrangement suited to your requirements.

Data & privacy

Know where your data goes.

We want processing location and data handling to be clear before you choose a model.

Singapore processing for Chinese models

Standard Chinese-model requests are sent to Singapore for inference. Australian or private deployment is a separate, tailored option for suitable open-source models.

No unrelated use of request content

Our gateway does not store model request content for marketing, resale or training our own models. Usage and billing metadata may be retained; diagnostic logs may contain request content when enabled for troubleshooting.

Supplier data terms

We are working to put appropriate data-security and handling terms in place with suppliers that process customer requests.

Location depends on the provider

Working with an Australian cloud partner does not by itself mean every international-model request is processed in Australia. Ask us to confirm the location and terms for your selected setup.

关于 YottaHop

YottaHop 面向澳洲客户提供统一的 AI 模型接入服务。我们帮助团队通过一个 API 和商业账户评估、接入和使用海外及中国模型，并提供模型选择、集成、用量管理和技术支持。

我们如何提供服务

海外模型方面，我们与澳洲本地云服务合作伙伴合作，提供有竞争力的价格及贴近客户的技术支持；实际处理地点取决于具体模型和供应商。标准服务中的中国模型目前在新加坡推理，请求数据需要传输至新加坡。对于适合的开源模型，如客户需要澳洲本地部署或企业私有化部署，我们也可以协助规划和实施。

数据与隐私

我们的网关不会为了营销、转售或训练自有模型等无关用途存储模型请求内容。运营所需的用量和账单元数据可能会保留；启用诊断日志时，请求内容可能出现在排障日志中。我们正与处理客户请求的供应商落实适当的数据安全和处理条款。更多信息请阅读完整隐私政策。
